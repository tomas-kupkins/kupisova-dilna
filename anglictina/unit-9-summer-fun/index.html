Tři budoucnosti = tři otázky

WILL

Tipuju?

Předpověď / názor.

I think it will rain tomorrow.

PRESENT CONTINUOUS

Mám domluveno?

Pevný plán nebo domluva.

We are meeting Tom at 5.

GOING TO

Mám v plánu?

Záměr / rozhodnutý plán.

I am going to camp this summer.
Pomůcka: WILL = tipuju • CONTINUOUS = mám domluveno • GOING TO = mám v plánu

will / won’t

subject + will + infinitive
We will have fun.
I won’t get lost.
Will you enjoy it?

Po will je vždy základní tvar: will go, will stay, will be.

Present continuous

am/is/are + verb-ing
I am meeting Jack tomorrow.
She is staying in a hotel next week.

Často uvidíš tomorrow, on Friday, next week…

be going to

am/is/are + going to + infinitive
He is going to travel.
They aren’t going to stay home.

Nezapomeň na am/is/are.

⚠️ He will goes ❌

He will go ✅

⚠️ I going to visit ❌

I am going to visit ✅
